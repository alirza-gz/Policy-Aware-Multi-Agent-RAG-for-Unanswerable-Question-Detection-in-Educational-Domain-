## Metric definitions

- **accuracy / precision / recall / f1** — binary unanswerable detection (positive = gold unanswerable; predicted positive = action ≠ ANSWER).
- **roc_auc / pr_auc** — from continuous `unanswerable_score = 1 - answerability_confidence` (never from hard labels).
- **coverage** — fraction of questions with action=ANSWER.
- **abstention_precision** — P(gold unanswerable | ABSTAIN).
- **abstention_recall** — P(ABSTAIN | gold unanswerable).
- **hallucination_rate / unsupported_answer_rate** — gold-unanswerable questions that received a substantive ANSWER.
- **policy_compliance_rate** — decision matches re-applied governance policy on recorded signals (not answer correctness).
- **retrieval metrics** — binary relevance vs `evidence_ids` (nDCG binary).
- **Agentic RAG** — honest single-agent proxy (reasoner self-governs); not an iterative tool-calling loop.

## 15. Limitations

- Corpus for educational_v2 is reconstructed from `evidence_passage` texts already in the JSONL (held-out passages excluded). Full Wikipedia article context beyond those evidence spans is not regenerated.
- nDCG uses binary relevance (no graded labels).
- Agentic RAG is a single-agent self-governance proxy, not iterative planning.
- Lexical groundedness is a proxy, not NLI entailment; LLM-judge metrics are not treated as objective ground truth.
- ROC/PR-AUC require continuous `answerability_confidence`; systems without meaningful variation may yield unstable AUC.
- Statistical significance does not imply practical importance; effect sizes are reported alongside p-values.
